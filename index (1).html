WhatsApp Cloud API

Bilingual Text & Rx Photo Intake

Normalizes incoming WhatsApp payloads, detects text vs. prescription images, downloads media URLs, and prepares structured prompts for Gemini 2.5 Flash.

Gemini 2.5 Flash

AI Extraction & Fuzzy Matching

Extracts medicine names and quantities from Arabic, English, or messy typos, then matches SKUs against the live Google Sheets Catalog with Levenshtein distance.

Telegram Bot Desk

Controlled Drug Approval Loop

Routes controlled items (e.g., Xanax, Night Calm) to the pharmacist Telegram group with inline Approve/Reject buttons that update stock and notify the patient.

Cron Operations

15-Minute Expiry Sweep & 22:00 Reorder

Every 15 minutes, overdue holds are marked expired and quantities automatically return to Catalog. Every night at 0 22 * * *, low-stock lists dispatch to Telegram and Gmail.

Reliability

Global Error Classification & Escalation

Captures failures via n8n Error Trigger, classifies Auth, Rate Limit, Timeout, or Parsing errors with suggested fixes, logs to Sheets, alerts Telegram, and emails admins on critical incidents.
