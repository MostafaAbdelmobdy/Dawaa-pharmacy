End-to-End Automation Architecture

Five coordinated n8n sub-pipelines running customer intake, clinical approvals, and inventory hygiene.

Dawaa 1 + Error Handling (Dawaa)
Pipeline 01

WhatsApp AI Intake & Vision OCR

Normalizes incoming English/Arabic WhatsApp text and prescription images. Calls Gemini 2.5 Flash to extract structured JSON medicine arrays, quantities, and prescription flags.

Pipeline 02

Smart Catalog Matching & Instant Reservation

Runs Levenshtein fuzzy matching against the live Google Sheets Catalog. Standard OTC orders auto-reserve instantly, deduct stock atomically, and send a WhatsApp pickup confirmation.

Pipeline 03

Pharmacist Human-in-the-Loop

Controlled drugs (Xanax, Lyrica) and prescriptions route to the Telegram Pharmacists group with one-tap Approve/Reject inline callbacks that update stock and notify the patient.

Pipeline 04

15-Min Expiry & Auto-Restock

Scheduled n8n cron sweeps Reservations every 15 minutes, marks overdue holds as expired, aggregates SKUs, returns units to Catalog stock, and notifies customers.

Pipeline 05

22:00 Reorder & Error Sentinel

Nightly low-stock digest dispatched to Telegram & Purchasing Gmail, paired with an Error Trigger that classifies failures, logs fingerprints to Sheets, and escalates critical incidents.
